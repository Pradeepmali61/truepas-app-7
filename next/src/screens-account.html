The short version
  • Your face is stored as an encrypted template on your phone.
  • Venues get a yes or no, never your face or documents.
  • You can see, download or erase everything.

01What we collect

Your name, contact details, the documents you add and an encrypted face template. We don’t keep photos of your face.

02How we use it

Only to confirm it’s you at places you’ve allowed, to prevent fraud, and to keep your account working.

03Who sees it

Venues receive a match result and the fields a check-in needs, such as your name and booking. Every share appears in your Privacy center.

04Your rights

You can access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent per industry, and reach our Grievance Officer within the app.
